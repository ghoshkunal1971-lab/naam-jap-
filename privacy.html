Privacy Policy

Naam Jap stores chant records so users can view their daily and lifetime progress. When a user signs in, records may be saved with Firebase Authentication and Firestore so the same account can access its data across devices.

We do not sell personal data. Account email is used only for sign in and record sync. Users can stop using the account feature and continue in local mode.

If advertising is enabled in the future, third-party vendors such as Google may use cookies to serve and measure ads according to their own policies.
